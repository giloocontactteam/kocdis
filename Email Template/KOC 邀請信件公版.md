**信件標題： 邀請加入 Giloo Impact Partner 計畫**

藍字為依據發信人與邀請單位調整內容

【稱呼】您好：

我是 Giloo 紀實影音的【姓名】。

【依對象客製，請參閱下述類別的文案】。希望能邀請您一起參與 Giloo Impact Partner 計畫！

Giloo (https://giloo.ist/zh-TW) 是來自台灣的電影串流與策展平台，專注於亞洲獨立電影、影展作品與紀錄片的策展與推廣，我們串聯導演、觀眾與機構，持續關注社會議題、政治運動與藝術創新，促進創作者與觀眾之間更深層的影像對話。Giloo 精彩作品如榮獲 2025 金馬獎最佳動畫短片並入圍威尼斯影展地平線單元的《螳螂》、魏德聖的《BIG》、經典動畫《魔法阿媽》等。

今年我們將進入北美市場，希望透過您的影響力，把這些在亞洲深受好評的作品介紹給北美觀眾。您在社群分享喜歡的作品，透過您帶來的訂閱或租借都能獲得分潤：

- 首筆月訂閱：每筆 USD 2
- 首筆年訂閱：每筆 USD 20
- 單片租借（TVOD）：未稅售價 15%
- 收益為每個月結算，累積達USD 100 即可申請提領，您可以隨時在後台查閱收益明細。

**參與方式如下，共四個步驟：**

1. 點選計畫頁面的「成為 Impact Partner」，註冊成為 Impact Partner：https://partner.giloo.ist/zh-TW
2. 從合作片單中挑選您想推薦的影片
3. 透過後台工具，產出您要發布的貼文與宣傳素材
4. 以您的專屬連結分享給觀眾

如果有任何問題，都歡迎聯繫我，期待您的回覆！

敬祝 平安健康

Giloo 紀實影音敬邀

**Email Title:** **Invitation to Join the Giloo Impact Partner Program**

Dear [Name],

My name is [Name], and I am writing on behalf of Giloo.

[Personalized line — customize per recipient using the matching category line below.] We would like to invite you to join the **Giloo Impact Partner Program**.

Giloo (https://giloo.ist/) is a Taiwan-based film streaming and curation platform dedicated to independent Asian cinema and festival films. We bring together filmmakers, audiences, and institutions through curated programs that highlight social issues, political movements, and contemporary artistic voices, fostering deeper dialogue between creators and viewers. Our catalog includes acclaimed, award-winning titles such as ***Praying Mantis*** (winner of the 2025 Golden Horse Award for Best Animated Short and an official selection in the Orizzonti section of the Venice Film Festival), Wei Te-sheng's ***BIG***, and the beloved animated classic ***Grandma and Her Ghosts***.

This year, Giloo is officially launching in North America, and we would like to invite you to collaborate with us in introducing our films — widely acclaimed across Asia — to North American audiences. When you share films you enjoy on Giloo with your community, you receive a share of the revenue generated by each subscription or rental you refer:

- First-time monthly subscription: USD 2 per referral
- First-time annual subscription: USD 20 per referral
- Single-title rental (TVOD): 15% of the pre-tax price

Earnings are settled on a monthly basis, and once your balance reaches USD 100, you may request a payout. A detailed breakdown of your earnings is available at any time through the dashboard.

**To participate, please follow these four steps:**

1. Register as an Impact Partner by clicking "Become an Impact Partner" on the program page: https://partner.giloo.ist/
2. Select the films you wish to recommend from our catalog
3. Generate posts and promotional materials using the dashboard tools
4. Share your dedicated referral link with your audience

Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us. We would be happy to assist. Looking forward to hearing from you!

Warm regards,

The Giloo Team
